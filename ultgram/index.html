VISUAL CONCEPT / NOT AN APP SCREENSHOT
01 / Built around choice

One messenger.
More ways to make it yours.

Ultgram brings selected ideas from popular alternative Telegram clients into one focused project. Beta 3.2 was installed and passed five device smoke tests; that does not yet make it a signed public release.

01 / CUSTOMIZATION

Appearance that feels personal

Theme presets, chat tab customization and practical navigation controls tested on Android devices.

02 / CHAT & MEDIA

Useful everyday extras

Chat translation, compatible story saving and one Downloads area for queue, history, cache and network settings.

03 / CONNECTIVITY

Strict proxy control

Locally managed SOCKS5/MTProto tools and a native Telegram-socket strict proxy guard. Not whole-device leak protection.

02 / Appearance, tested and refined

Pick your era.

A range of visual directions, including Candy Pink, Midnight and Neon Stage. Internal builds have been used on Pixel and Samsung devices; appearance refinements continue.

03 / Practical privacy, clear limits

Control without the mystique.

Internal beta, not a store release

A FOSS ARM64 candidate passed build and device smoke tests. Public release signing and distribution checks are still in progress.

Know what a proxy does

The strict guard was tested for Telegram native sockets, but cannot prove whole-app or whole-device anonymity. Proxies do not make your Telegram account anonymous to Telegram.

Honest feature boundaries

Client-side appearance and chat extras do not unlock paid server-side Premium entitlements. DoQ/DoH3 and tracker controls are not active protections in the beta.

SMS/RCS is on hold

SMS/MMS companion and RCS handoff research is excluded from the current APK. Third-party full RCS support is not promised.
